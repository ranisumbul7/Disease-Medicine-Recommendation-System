About Sumbul Rani
Meet the Developer

Hello, I'm Sumbul Rani, a computer science student with an interest in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. I enjoy learning new technologies and applying them to real-world problems through practical projects.

My Learning Journey

I am currently exploring different areas of technology such as Python programming, web development, and machine learning. Through academic projects and practice, I am building my skills and understanding how intelligent systems can be developed.

This Personalized Healthcare Recommendation System is developed as a learning project to demonstrate how Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning can be applied in the healthcare field. The system allows users to enter their symptoms and analyzes the information using a trained machine learning model to predict possible diseases. After prediction, the platform provides useful health-related suggestions such as precautions, medicines, diet plans, and workout recommendations. The goal of this project is to make basic health information more accessible and to show how technology can assist people in understanding their health conditions.

Interest in Health Technology

I am very interested in the field of health technology and how Artificial Intelligence can support healthcare services. With the help of AI and data analysis, it is possible to build systems that can assist users by providing quick health insights and guidance. Through this project, I explored how machine learning models can analyze symptoms and generate useful suggestions. This project helped me understand how technology can be used to improve health awareness and make healthcare information easier to access for everyone.
